Air Freight Services

Time-sensitive air cargo forwarding with international carrier coordination.

Air Cargo Forwarding

Sky Freightor Logistic provides air freight forwarding services for businesses that require time-sensitive shipment of goods. We coordinate with international air carriers to move your cargo efficiently through global aviation networks.

Our air freight service handles shipment booking, carrier selection, documentation preparation, and cargo coordination — ensuring your consignment is processed and dispatched according to schedule.

Time-Sensitive Shipments

For urgent consignments, air freight offers the fastest transit times. We work with major airlines and cargo carriers to secure capacity and schedule shipments that meet your delivery timelines.

What We Coordinate

  • Air cargo booking and carrier coordination
  • Shipment documentation and airway bill preparation
  • Airport cargo handling and dispatch
  • Import and export customs documentation
  • Shipment tracking and status updates
  • Door-to-door pickup and delivery arrangements

International Cargo Movement

Our air freight forwarding covers international cargo routes, coordinating with airlines, ground handlers, and customs authorities at origin and destination.

Door-to-Door Possibilities

For air freight shipments, we can arrange origin pickup and destination delivery — providing a door-to-door service option where the complete logistics chain is managed by our team.

Sea Freight Services

Container shipping and international sea cargo forwarding.

FCL — Full Container Load

For shipments that fill an entire container, our FCL service provides dedicated container space for your cargo. We coordinate with shipping lines to secure vessel space, manage documentation, and arrange port handling.

LCL — Less than Container Load

When your cargo does not require a full container, our LCL service allows you to share container space with other shipments — a cost-effective option for smaller consignments.

Import & Export Cargo

We handle both import and export sea freight shipments. Whether bringing goods into Pakistan or shipping cargo to international destinations, our team manages the documentation and carrier coordination.

Container Freight Forwarding

  • FCL and LCL container booking
  • Shipping line coordination and vessel scheduling
  • Container loading and discharge arrangements
  • Bill of lading and shipping documentation
  • Port and terminal coordination
  • Freight rate negotiation and quotation

Port Coordination

Our team coordinates with port authorities, terminal operators, and shipping agents at origin and destination ports.

Door-to-Door Logistics

We can arrange origin pickup, container loading, ocean transportation, port clearance, and final destination delivery — providing a complete door-to-door logistics solution.

Customs Clearance Services

Import and export documentation support and customs coordination.

Customs Clearance Support

Sky Freightor Logistic provides customs clearance services to support the import and export of goods. Our team assists with documentation preparation, submission, and coordination with customs authorities.

Import Documentation Support

For import shipments, we assist with import declarations, duty assessment support, and coordination with relevant regulatory bodies.

Export Documentation Support

For export shipments, we handle export declaration preparation, documentation for destination country requirements, and customs coordination at departure.

What We Assist With

  • Import and export declaration preparation
  • Documentation review and submission
  • Customs duty and tax assessment support
  • Coordination with customs authorities
  • Regulatory documentation compliance
  • Shipment release and clearance follow-up

Shipment Documentation Assistance

We assist with commercial invoices, packing lists, certificates of origin, and other trade documents required for customs clearance and international trade compliance.

Warehousing Services

Cargo receiving, storage, and distribution support.

Our Warehouse Facility

Sky Freightor Logistic operates a warehouse facility in Karachi for cargo receiving, storage, and distribution. The warehouse supports our freight forwarding operations.

Cargo Receiving

We receive incoming cargo at our warehouse for consolidation, storage, or distribution. Cargo is checked, documented, and prepared for onward transportation.

Storage

Our warehouse provides storage space for cargo awaiting customs clearance, onward transportation, or distribution.

What We Provide

  • Cargo receiving and inspection
  • Secure storage arrangements
  • Inventory and shipment documentation
  • Cargo consolidation for export
  • De-consolidation for import shipments
  • Distribution coordination and dispatch

Distribution Support

From our warehouse, we coordinate cargo distribution to delivery points including scheduling dispatch and organizing delivery routes.

Shipment Handling

Our team handles cargo at the warehouse with professional care — including loading, unloading, labeling, and preparation for transportation.
