About Our Company

Delivering Confidence Across Every Shipment

SKYFREIGHTOR LOGESTIC PVT LTD is a freight forwarding and logistics company based in Karachi, Pakistan, supporting businesses that need their cargo planned, coordinated, and delivered with care. From the first booking to the final handover, our team works closely with customers to arrange dependable air, sea, and road freight solutions shaped around each shipment.

Every consignment is different. That is why we combine practical shipment planning, careful cargo handling, and clear, consistent communication — so you always know where your freight stands and what happens next. Whether you are importing materials, exporting products, or moving goods between markets, we coordinate the carriers, documentation, and timelines that keep your supply chain moving.

  • Customer-focused logistics support
  • Air and sea freight solutions
  • Road transportation coordination
  • Cargo handling and shipment planning
  • Import and export support
  • Reliable communication throughout the shipping process

Working with SKYFREIGHTOR LOGESTIC PVT LTD means having a single, accountable partner for your freight. We arrange bookings, prepare and check documentation, coordinate with carriers and port authorities, and stay reachable at every stage of the movement — so the responsibility for coordination sits with us, not with you.

Our approach is practical and transparent: we confirm what is possible before your cargo moves, we explain costs clearly, and we keep you informed from pickup to final delivery. That is how we build shipping relationships that last.

What We Do

Our Logistics Services

Reliable freight and supply-chain support across every major mode — planned, coordinated, and communicated by one accountable team.

Air Freight

Time-critical shipments moved through trusted airline partnerships, with coordinated uplift, documentation, and arrival handling at major gateway airports.

Sea Freight

Cost-efficient ocean transport for full and partial consignments, planned around dependable sailing schedules and professional port handling.

Road Freight

Dependable trucking coordination connecting ports, airports, warehouses, and customer premises across national and cross-border routes.

International Freight Forwarding

End-to-end coordination of your shipment across carriers, documents, and deadlines — one accountable partner from origin to destination.

Full Container Load (FCL)

Dedicated full containers for larger consignments — secure, sealed, and moved on schedules built around your production and delivery windows.

Less than Container Load (LCL)

Flexible shared-container space for smaller volumes, with careful consolidation and de-consolidation to protect cargo and control cost.

Customs Clearance

Preparation and lodgement of import and export documentation with prompt, compliant processing to keep cargo moving through the port.

Warehousing & Storage

Organised, secure storage with systematic receipt, stacking, and stock handling — ready for staging, stuffing, or onward distribution.

Cargo Handling

Professional loading, unloading, lashing, and shifting of cargo with the right equipment and experienced supervision at every step.

Distribution Services

Structured onward movement of goods from hub to market, with planned delivery rounds and clear communication at each handover.

Import & Export Logistics

Complete import and export support — documentation, coordination, and compliance handled correctly the first time, in both directions of trade.

Transit Cargo

Coordination of cargo moving in transit through regional trade corridors — bonded documentation, carrier liaison, and supervised movement from port of discharge to the final destination.

Door-to-Door Shipping

One booking covers everything: pickup at origin, international movement, clearance, and final delivery at the consignee's door — worldwide.
